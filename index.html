How does the math work? ⌄

At each usable gauge, compare the latest observed water level against NOAA's predicted water level at that same station and time, both in feet relative to its local MLLW datum. Transfer a distance-, region-, and freshness-weighted residual to the selected NOAA prediction baseline. The estimated correction fades into the future. Outliers and old observations are rejected.

Important: basins use approximate regions, not traced waterways; the method is experimental and has no calibrated accuracy bounds. NOAA's local MLLW datum is not necessarily identical to the datum at the selected beach.
